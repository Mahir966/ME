        Biography & Direction
        
          
        I am a curious learner, developer, and aspiring leader driven by one simple idea: never stop becoming better than who I was yesterday. My interests exist at the intersection of technology, strategy, human behavior, leadership, and entrepreneurship. I do not want to be defined by a single skill or a single field; I want to understand systems, people, and ideas—and turn that understanding into something meaningful.
        
          
        My journey began with hands-on experimentation. Over time, that curiosity evolved into a deeper commitment to programming, cybersecurity, digital forensics, and client-side systems engineering. Projects, experiments, and problem-solving have become my most valuable teachers. Beyond technology, I maintain long-term ambitions connected with discipline, service, and strategic leadership.
